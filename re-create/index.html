Re-CREATE: Participatory Environmental Modeling

05.1

About the Work

Format
Participatory installation and interactive environmental modeling
Context
Art and Science Symposium, Aalborg University, Denmark, 2023

Re-CREATE turns the gallery into a collaborative studio where visitors arrange physical objects inside a light-controlled miniature box to design shared environments. Participants pair their physical arrangements with an AI-generated background and custom programmable LED lighting before photographing their room to join a collective digital house. Combining hands-on miniature making with responsive lighting turns environmental design into a playful, social activity across gallery visitors.
