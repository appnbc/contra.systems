Amerihcc Licc: Bio-Ethics and Chimerical Metaphors

Watch Video Excerpt ↗
07.1

About the Work

Format
Video installation and projection mapping
Context
TTTLabs, Crete, Greece, 2024

Amerihcc Licc examines genetic identity and cultural heritage through projection mapping and generative visual patterns. This single-channel video installation blends organic biomaterial imagery with woven textile structures, creating a luminous visual surface that explores biological hybridity and material memory.
