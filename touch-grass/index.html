Touch Grass: A Nostalgic Picnic and the Reclamation of Space

06.1

About the Work

Format
Experimental installation and spatial media
Context
Tropical Lab, Singapore, 2023

Touch Grass stages an indoor picnic inside the gallery using synthetic turf, gingham textiles, woven baskets, and interactive digital tags. Bringing outdoor leisure materials into an exhibition setting invites visitors to step onto a shared tactile surface, sit together, and explore how sensory textures and digital prompts spark conversation, nostalgia, and connection among strangers.
